Packaging/Appearance/Accessories: Solid box, easy to open; the plain brown cardboard inside is fine by me. No dents or scratches, and the protective film peeled off easily. Solid and a bit heavy (the big battery, I assume). Everything included except the QSG, which I downloaded.

App pairing & Wi-Fi: Simple. Add Device with the QR code for both doorbell and chime, joined directly to my home IoT network (told not to use my HomeBase Professional S1 for now). Doorbell firmware update went smoothly.

Chime pairing: Blue light was slow to come on at first plug-in. Pairing worked, but the firmware update sat on "updating" for 5+ minutes. A power cycle fixed it (now FW 1.0.1.5) and it rings when the doorbell is pressed. Update, hours later: the app shows the T8028 chime as "Updating..." again (red "device updating" in the device page) and Restart Device can't be clicked.

Installation, battery (first install): Very easy. Holes matched my old Eufy doorbell exactly. Used screws with both the bracket and wedge, no issues.

Installation, wired (second install): Noticeably harder:
Wires have to enter through the angle bracket's lower opening, up a channel, then through the main bracket's upper-only opening. I needed three hands: the top screw goes through both brackets, so the wires must be fed first, and working room was tight. I spliced pigtails to my doorbell wires and flattened them so it could close.
The doorbell went in with effort and no satisfying "click". I couldn't pull it out by hand, but re-seated it a few times to be sure.

Power: Running on battery. My transformer turns out to be on a switched outlet, so it loses power when the stair light switch is off. D'oh! On the plus side, the app notified me of the power loss and the doorbell switched to battery on its own, which is how I found it. I'll report back on wired power once it's fixed.

Suggestion: Match the wire openings on the angle and regular brackets so wires pass straight through both.
